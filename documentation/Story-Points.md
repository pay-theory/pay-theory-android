# Story Points 

Pay Theory uses story points to estimate and track the development work being done

The idea is to make an estimate for each category below, add them all together and then take the average as your estimated story points for a Jira ticket


# Familiarity

1 \= Understand all tasks, I do this daily

2 \= Understand most tasks, I have done this before

3 \= Understand some tasks, it’s been awhile since I have done this

5 \= Understand few tasks, I’ve never done THIS but have done similar

8 \= Unsure of tasks, I’ve never done it or anything like it (NEEDS DISCOVERY)  

# Complexity

1 \= Could do it in my sleep, don’t anticipate any roadblocks

2 \= Need to be awake and aware but don’t anticipate many, if any, roadblocks

3 \= It’s will be tough and require a lot of my attentions, there are anticipated roadblocks

5 \= This will be hard, require a lot of focus and I plan to encounter several roadblocks

8 \= Unsure of the complexity (NEEDS DISCOVERY)

# Time

1 \= Minutes 

2 \= Hours 

3 \= A day or two 

5 \= A few days to a week 

8 \= Longer than a Sprint (NEEDS BROKEN DOWN)

# Risk 

	1 \= No risk

2 \= Maybe some risk

3 \= Moderate risk

5 \= High Risk 

8 \= Unclear Risks (NEEDS DISCOVERY)

# Effort

1 \= A crisp & refreshing task that will actually help me build momentum  

2 \= I will feel accomplished and make good progress on our goals 

3 \= Will likely wear me down and leave little space for other tasks

5 \= Will likely wear me out and be my entire focus this Sprint

8 \= This is too big to take on alone (NEEDS BROKEN DOWN)
